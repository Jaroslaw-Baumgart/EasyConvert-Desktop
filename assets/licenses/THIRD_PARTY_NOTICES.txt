Poppler 26.09.0 and dependencies
------------------------------
Poppler is licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later. It is invoked as a separate
command-line program. Its DLLs, fonts and other dependencies have their own
licenses, preserved under resources/poppler/third-party/.

Package URLs, versions, SHA256 hashes, source URLs and source hashes are in

Corresponding source archives and recipes are provided as
